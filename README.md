Velvet Vault — an original top-down stealth heist game for make.fun's best-heist-game-20261122-0559 bounty. Slip through three night rooms — the Gallery, the Vault Hall, the Rooftop — past patrolling guards and sweeping security cameras, lift every gem to unlock the exit, and reach the getaway van. A vision cone that catches you fills the alarm meter; three catches and the job is blown. Smoke bombs blind every eye for a few seconds, at a price. Keyboard (WASD/arrows, Shift to sprint, Space for smoke) and mouse/touch (click to walk), WebAudio sound synthesised in code, no files, no network, no build step.
